# Running webinar recaps and article recaps

The way to run webinar recaps, article recaps is as follows:

- Company holds this view, so write it as Company's position in Company's voice. Company's own blog post is the evidence, not the subject.
- Name Person once, in the intro or the first timeline sentence, as the person who built one. The timeline stays in their words because they lived it. Don't repeat their name per stage.
- Use their words as pull quotes, not attributed paraphrase. Put the quote in the sentence and let the link do the crediting. For example: The dangerous moment isn't when it fails. It's when it works "just well enough to convince everyone they should continue managing the MMM themselves." with the link on the phrase.
- Do not use "X says," "as X describes it," or "in X's view" as a sentence opener. Support claims with a short direct quote plus an inline link instead.
- Name a person only on first mention and when their experience is the point.
